## 1. Dosa + Paneer — 2×/week

- 80 g high-protein paneer

- 250 g id dosa batter(water 55%, rice 34 %, urad dal, fenugreek, and iodised salt) x 2
- ~80 g spicy cheese dip x 2


**Total serving size:** ~410 g

### Spicy Cheese Dip — 3 Servings
- 60 g processed cheese
- 200 ml milk
- 11 g garlic
- 15 g pickled jalapeños
- ¼ tsp smoked paprika



**Approx. per meal:** ~590 kcal · ~31 g protein · ~75 g carbs · ~16 g fat · ~2 g fiber

## 2. Bun Kebab + Paneer — 2×/week

- 80 g high-protein paneer

- 55g kidney beans
- 50g soya granules
- 50g green peas
- 180 g onion + 4 green chillies + 15gm garlic, sautéed till red
- 10-15gm besan + 1.5 tbsp garam masala + 3 tsp dhaniya powder+ 3 tsp red chilli powder + 1/2 tsp turmeric + 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 pairs of bun x 2
- (1.5 tbsp Hellmann's Veg Mayo + 1 tsp Wingreens Tandoori Mayo) x2

**Total serving size:** ~420 g by listed ingredient weight; prepared weight varies after soaking and cooking

**Approx. per meal:** ~805–855 kcal · ~51–55 g protein · ~99–110 g carbs · ~24 g fat · ~16–20 g fiber

## 3. Pink Sauce Pasta

- 80 g high-protein paneer

- 4gm garlic + 2tsp red chilli powder + 1 tsp dhania powder
- 20-30g Wingreens pizza pasta sauce
- 40-60 gm pasta
- 20g capcicum
- 1 serving of spicy cheese dip

**Total serving size:** ~250–280 g

**Approx. per meal:** ~440–520 kcal · ~33–36 g protein · ~47–63 g carbs · ~15 g fat · ~5–6 g fiber
