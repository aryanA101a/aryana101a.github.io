## 1. Dosa + Paneer — 3×/week

- 160 g plain dosa
- 100 g paneer
- ~60 g spicy cheese dip
- 1 medium raw carrot

**Total serving size:** ~390 g

### Spicy Cheese Dip — 3 Servings
- 70 g processed cheese
- 90–100 ml milk
- 5 g garlic
- 15 g pickled jalapeños
- 8–10 g jalapeño brine
- ¼ tsp smoked paprika

Blend milk, garlic, most jalapeños, brine, and paprika. Heat gently for 3–4 min, melt in cheese on low heat, then stir in remaining chopped jalapeños. Divide into **3 × ~60 g**.

**Approx. per meal:** ~650–660 kcal · ~32 g protein · ~56–57 g carbs · ~34 g fat · ~4 g fiber

## 2. Hummus + Paneer — 2×/week

- ~150 g homemade hummus
- 100 g raw paneer
- 40 g onion, sautéed
- 20 g capsicum, sautéed
- 1 medium raw carrot
- Small amount of butter for sautéing

**Total serving size:** ~380–385 g

### Hummus — 2 Servings
- 120 g dry chickpeas
- 2.4 tbsp olive oil
- 4–6 g garlic
- 20–30 g lemon juice
- Cold water as needed
- Baking soda for soaking/cooking

Prepare ~300 g hummus and divide into **2 × ~150 g**.

**Approx. per meal:** ~680–720 kcal · ~31 g protein · ~51 g carbs · ~41–45 g fat · ~13–14 g fiber

*Approximate values assuming a ~70 g carrot and standard full-fat paneer/processed cheese. The hummus range allows for ~0–5 g butter; actual values vary by brand and preparation.*

---

## Storage

- Store **hummus and cheese dip separately** in clean, airtight containers at **≤4°C**.
- Refrigerate within **2 hours** of preparation.
- Use within **3–4 days**.
