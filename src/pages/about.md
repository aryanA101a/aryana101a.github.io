Hey, I’m Aryan — a software engineer who enjoys building systems close to the metal.

Most of my interests orbit around low-level programming, operating systems, networking, distributed systems, and performance-oriented software. I enjoy understanding how things work internally, whether that means reading source code at 2 AM or building tools from scratch just to understand the tradeoffs firsthand.

Lately, I’ve been exploring systems software, HTTP clients, bootloader networking, open source infrastructure, and the occasional rabbit hole that absolutely did not need to become a three-day investigation.

Outside programming, I spend time gaming, reading about Adhyatm and self-realization, and dreaming up projects with a questionable amount of engineering for the fun of it.

This site is where I write about software, systems, experiments, and whatever currently has my terminal hostage.
